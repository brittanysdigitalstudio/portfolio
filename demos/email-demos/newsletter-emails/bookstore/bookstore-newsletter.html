Weekly Happenings

Things that will be happening every week this month.

💅🔢 Week 1 - Drag Bingo

Warm up your bingo skills because bingo will be happening. Bingo isn't just for old people, but we prefer to keep it to adults only. Only adults 18+ can come. We just don't want to be free baby sitters.

🌻 Week 2 - Native Plants

This week is the Douglas Spira. We will be taking a virtual tour through a public garden via drone and learn all about the Douglas Spira.

📖 Week 3 - Kids Story Time

Gather your kids, and make sure to stay, as we read Fairy Land Creature. A new award winning kids book. We will have our own live prince and princess, along with a special guest.

🍷 Week 4 - Sip and Shop

Our monthly after hours sip and shop. We will have snacks made from locals throughout the store while you shop our books. We will have a few local authors here for a mini book signing event as well! Browse the local indie authors, and drink some yummy wine.
